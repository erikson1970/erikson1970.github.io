Exotic heat engines

Engines and refrigerators that run on sound, bubbles and other unlikely things.

  • Thermoacoustics Labv1.0

    Heat engines and refrigerators where a sound wave replaces the pistons. A singing Rijke tube; a loudspeaker-driven standing-wave refrigerator like the one that flew on the Space Shuttle in 1992 and Penn State's Ben & Jerry's ice-cream chiller; a standing-wave engine; the Los Alamos traveling-wave Stirling engine with its jet-pump "gas diode"; and the gas-fired machine that burns part of a natural-gas stream to liquefy the rest.

Standing-wave devices use linear short-stack theory; the traveling-wave engine and the liquefier are lumped models calibrated to published machines.

Each page is a single self-contained file that runs in the browser: no server, no install.
