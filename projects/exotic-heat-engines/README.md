# Exotic heat engines

Heat engines and refrigerators off the beaten track. Served at
`erikson1970.github.io/projects/exotic-heat-engines/`.

## Contents

```text
index.html            section page
thermoacoustics/      Thermoacoustics Lab v1.0
```

`thermoacoustics/index.html` is one self-contained file (Google Fonts is the
only external load). It has five machine types:

- a Rijke tube
- a loudspeaker-driven standing-wave refrigerator, with examples after Tijani et al. (2002), the 1992 Space Shuttle STAR and the 2004 Ben & Jerry's chiller
- a standing-wave prime mover
- the Backhaus & Swift (1999) traveling-wave thermoacoustic Stirling engine
- the LANL/Praxair gas-fired thermoacoustic natural-gas liquefier

Standing-wave stacks use the short-stack boundary-layer theory of Swift (1988),
normalized as in Tijani et al. (2002). The traveling-wave engine and the liquefier
are lumped models calibrated to the published machines.

The page was developed in the separate `Energy-Flow` working folder
(`visualization/thermoacoustics.html`) and copied here with a breadcrumb added.
