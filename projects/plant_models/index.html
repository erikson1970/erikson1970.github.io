Plant models

Thermal plants you can run in the browser, from a single heat exchanger to a reactor plant.

Heat exchangers & storage

  • Counterflow heat exchangerv1.0

    One double-pipe exchanger with inlet temperatures and flow rates you can drive in either direction: rating and sizing modes, ε-NTU and LMTD, and a finite-volume transient that settles onto the analytic profile.

  • Exchanger & storage tank loopv2.0

    A cooling-water exchanger and a recuperator feeding a stratified storage tank with surface heat loss, a mid-height sample valve, and a button to swap the two exchanger units.

  • Three-exchanger tank loopv3.1

    Adds a third exchanger with its own service stream, primary inlet temperatures up to 300 °C, and a switch that puts the recuperator before or after the cooler.

Pressurized-water reactor plant

  • PWR plant labv4.0

    A single-loop PWR: point kinetics with moderator feedback, two-speed coolant pumps with check valves, pressurizer heaters and spray, steam generator, propulsion turbine, two turbine generators and a seawater-cooled condenser.

  • PWR plant labv5.0

    Six-group kinetics with Doppler, moderator and xenon feedback, decay heat, rod control, steam-generator level, a sampling loop with charging and discharge, shore power, metric/imperial units and four operator panels.

  • PWR plant labv5.1

    v5.0 with a loop electric plant: port and starboard vital and non-vital AC buses, a tie bus with shore power, motor-generators to a DC bus and battery, all switched from a clickable one-line diagram.

All values are generic, textbook-scale figures chosen for teaching. No real plant’s design data is used or implied.

Each page is a single self-contained file that runs in the browser: no server, no install.
