Reactor plants you can run, the famous ones that failed, and a few that only exist in stories.

Nuclear power plants

  • PWR plant labv4.0

    A single-loop PWR: point kinetics with moderator feedback, two-speed coolant pumps with check valves, pressurizer heaters and spray, steam generator, propulsion turbine, two turbine generators and a seawater-cooled condenser.

  • PWR plant labv5.0

    Six-group kinetics with Doppler, moderator and xenon feedback, decay heat, rod control, steam-generator level, a sampling loop with charging and discharge, shore power, metric/imperial units and four operator panels.

  • PWR plant labv5.2

    The v5.1 plant with its loop electric plant, plus a limit board: every trip, fast rod insertion, rod stop and automatic action with live values and overrides; smarter arming during scram recovery; maximum power by pump line-up; a larger pressurizer and charging system; regular and fast rod speeds. Replaces v5.1.

  • PWR plant labv6.0

    A low-subcooling PWR at the same 150 MW thermal: jet pumps driven by smaller drive pumps instead of canned coolant pumps, a raised steam generator for natural circulation, subcooled boiling with a hot-channel DNBR, spring-assisted check valves, ejector cavitation and NPSH limits, and a power–flow map with a stability limit. Lose the drive pumps and watch it ride down onto natural circulation.
